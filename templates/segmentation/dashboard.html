AI Segmentation Workflow

End-to-end medical deep learning inference pipeline

01 MRI Ingestion

Grayscale axial brain MRI scans (PNG, JPG) are validated, resized with bilinear aspect-ratio padding to 256×256, and normalized to [0, 1].

02 U-Net Segmentation

The trained convolutional model processes feature maps across encoder-decoder skip connections, applying a decision threshold of 0.40.

03 Interactive Analysis

High-contrast binary masks and red composite overlays are generated and made available in an interactive comparison viewer.
