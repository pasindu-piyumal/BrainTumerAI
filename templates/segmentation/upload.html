MRI Acquisition Guidelines

Axial T1/T2/FLAIR Orientation
Standard axial brain slices yield the most accurate segmentation predictions with the trained U-Net.
Aspect-Ratio Preserving Resize
Uploaded scans are automatically padded and scaled to 256×256 pixels to preserve anatomical proportions without distortion.
Calibrated 0.40 Decision Threshold
The model applies a calibrated probability cutoff of 0.40 optimized during validation to maximize the Dice similarity coefficient.
